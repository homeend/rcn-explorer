      What the data is

      Every record is a sale taken from a notarial deed and entered in the Rejestr Cen Nieruchomości by the county office (starostwo). Since 13 Feb 2026 the register is free and public. It is served by GUGiK as a WFS at mapy.geoportal.gov.pl/wss/service/rcn with layers dzialki (parcels), budynki (buildings) and lokale (units). This app groups the records of one deed into one transaction.

      Your browser downloads the raw register data through a small relay on this site (the register itself does not allow direct browser access), then parses and groups it locally. Downloaded areas are kept in your browser for 7 days.

      Reading the numbers

      • Price is the gross price of the whole transaction. One deed can cover several parcels, a house plus garage, or a flat plus a parking space.
      • PLN/m² is calculated only when the area is clear: one flat (usable area), one house (usable area, often missing) or empty land (sum of the parcel areas).
      • New vs resale flats come from the rodzaj rynku field. In suburbs, many "new flats" are semi-detached or terraced homes registered as units.
      • Counties upload data with a delay, so the last few months are incomplete. Older years (before about 2010) are often sparse.
      • Sales without a free-market flag (auctions, municipal sales, family transfers) and sales of partial shares distort prices. They are hidden by default.

      Map layers

      The layer switcher on the map adds GUGiK orthophoto, cadastral parcels (KIEG, visible when zoomed in), local zoning plans (MPZP) and the official RCN map layer. These are drawn directly by Geoportal's WMS services.
